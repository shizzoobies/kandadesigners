The tradeoff. Option A ranks everything into one stream, so there is exactly one place to look and money always sits at the top. It scales badly past roughly twenty items. Option B keeps each rule in its own box, which is easier to scan when several things are busy and easier to add a seventh rule to later, but nothing forces the money to the top and empty panels take up space on quiet days.
