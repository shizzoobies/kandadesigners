## Web design — what matters and why

- Speed is revenue. Slow sites lose visitors before they see anything; every second of load time measurably increases bounce and lowers conversion. K & A builds static-first, image-optimized, motion-efficient sites that score well on Core Web Vitals.
- Custom beats template. Template sites look like everyone else's, carry code bloat that slows them down, and fight you when the business needs something specific. A purpose-built site is designed around the one job it must do — booking patients, capturing leads, selling memberships.
- Design with intent. Good sites use restraint: one coherent visual system, generous whitespace, real content presented plainly, motion with meaning. Text is never placed over busy imagery without a solid backdrop.
- Mobile first. Most local-business traffic is on phones; layouts, tap targets, and performance are designed for small screens before large ones.
- Accessibility is not optional. Roughly one in four adults lives with a disability. Accessible sites reach more customers, rank better, and reduce legal risk (ADA website lawsuits target small businesses regularly). Accessibility done right is invisible to everyone it doesn't help.

## SEO — how being found actually works

- Technical SEO is the foundation: crawlable pages, clean URLs, sitemaps, canonical tags, fast loads, mobile rendering. If machines can't read the site, nothing else matters.
- Structured data (schema.org markup) tells search engines exactly what a business is — services, hours, location, reviews — and unlocks rich results.
- Content architecture: each page should answer one clear question well. Clear headings, plain language, and genuine expertise beat keyword stuffing every time.
- Local SEO: for Florida businesses, a complete Google Business Profile, consistent name/address/phone across the web, and location-specific pages matter enormously.
- SEO is a build-quality issue first. Most SEO problems are baked in at build time — slow pages, broken semantics, missing metadata. K & A builds sites SEO-ready from day one instead of bolting it on later.

## AI search / answer-engine readiness (AEO)

- The shift: a growing share of customers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overviews instead of scanning ten blue links. The AI gives one synthesized answer — businesses are either in it or invisible.
- Being quotable: answer engines favor pages with clear, factual, well-structured statements about who a business serves, what it does, and what makes it different. Vague marketing copy gets skipped; specific claims get quoted.
- Structured data helps machines cite you. The same schema.org markup that powers rich results helps AI systems attribute facts to your business.
- Fast, accessible, semantic sites win twice: the qualities that make a site rank on Google — speed, clean markup, real content — are the same qualities AI crawlers reward.
- K & A builds "AI-answer ready" sites: content architected as clear questions and answers, structured data throughout, and factual pages an answer engine can lift and attribute.
