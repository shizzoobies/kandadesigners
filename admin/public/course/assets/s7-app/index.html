Same skills, new front door

What stays the same

  • You sign in once with your Anthropic account.
  • You ask in plain English.
  • It reads and edits your real files.
  • You review every change before it sticks.
  • Connectors are still MCP, added once.

What is new

  • A window instead of a terminal.
  • Click and scroll instead of typing commands.
  • Drag files in to share them.
  • Your chats and projects are saved for you.
  • Switch models and settings with a menu.
