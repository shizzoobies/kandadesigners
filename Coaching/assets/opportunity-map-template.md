## 1. Business snapshot

**What the business does, in their words:**
> One or two sentences. If they can't say it in two, that's worth noting too.

**Team size and who does what:**
> Owner plus how many? Who touches the admin work: quotes, inbox, scheduling, invoices?

**Tools they already use:**
> Email provider, calendar, docs or Drive, invoicing, CRM if any, point of sale. List what's real, not what they signed up for once.

**Where their files and customer info actually live:**
> Inbox? Drive? A binder? Their head? Be honest here, it decides what's buildable.

## 4. The chosen build

The one workflow weeks 3 through 8 target.

**What we're building:**
> Name it plainly. "A quoting assistant that drafts estimates from your job notes," not "AI transformation."

**Why this one and not the others:**
> Usually: highest hours touched, buildable in their existing tools, and they'll actually use it weekly. Say which of those it was.

**What done looks like at the end of week 8:**
> One sentence. Something running in their business that was not there before.

**What we're deliberately not building yet:**
> The runners-up stay on the list. They become the week 9 to 12 self-build candidates and the written plan at handoff.

---

## 5. The quick win shipped today

Every first session ships something so they feel it immediately.

**What it was:**
> Example: a project set up in their account that drafts customer replies in their voice, or a prompt that turns job notes into a quote draft.

**Where it lives:**
> Which project in their account, what it's called, how they get back to it tomorrow without help.

**How they'll use it this week:**
> One specific task they agreed to run through it before the next call.

## 6. Account setup checklist

Everything is theirs from day one. Their subscription, their card, their name, their accounts.

- [ ] Claude subscription created on their own card, under their own name, billed by Anthropic directly
- [ ] They know their own login and where billing lives, no credentials shared in either direction
- [ ] First project created in their account, named for their business
- [ ] Project description written (see the starter pack)
- [ ] Any business accounts the build needs (email, Drive, etc.) confirmed as theirs and accessible by them
- [ ] Confirmed: nothing in this program runs through K&A's accounts, and everything built here stays in theirs
