## 4. What to do when it breaks

**Try these before emailing Alex, in order:**

1. Re-read the output. Most "it broke" moments are one unclear instruction. Rephrase what you asked and run it again.
2. Check the project. Is the right project selected? Did the project description or files change?
3. Check your inputs. Wrong file, empty paste, or a format it hasn't seen before will produce junk. Garbage in still applies.
4. Start a fresh conversation in the project. Long conversations drift. Fresh chat, same ask.
5. Check your subscription and login. Billing hiccups on your card show up as the tool not responding.

**Then email Alex.** Include: what you asked, what you got, what you expected. A screenshot beats a description.
