# Plan Selection Guide

One page for the first call. The job on this call is to pick right, not to close big. A bad fit refunded costs more than a good "not yet."

**On the table today, and only these:**

| | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 90-Day AI Launch | $2,000, or 3 payments of $700 | Twelve weeks, weekly working sessions, one real workflow running in their business by the end. Built in their own accounts, theirs to keep. |
| Launch + Site | $3,999 | The same twelve weeks, plus a full custom site built by K&A alongside. The site is built to K&A's standard, not co-built. Sessions teach the layer they operate: their copy, their inquiries, their pages. |

**What is not on the table today:** the ongoing membership. It exists, and it is offered in week 10 of the program, when the member can see what they'd lose by stopping. It is never sold on the first call. If they ask about ongoing help, the answer is: "There's an optional way to keep working together after the ninety days. We'll talk about it near the end, when you know what it's worth to you."

---

## The four questions that decide it

**1. "What eats your week that you know shouldn't take that long?"**

- A specific, recurring answer (quotes, the inbox, scheduling, paperwork): there's something to build. Continue.
- "Nothing really" or purely one-off problems: probably not yet. A twelve-week program needs a weekly problem.

**2. "What does your website do for you right now?"**

- No site, an embarrassing site, or a site they can't touch: Launch + Site. A site is something they already know they want, and building it alongside the program means the workflow and the site feed each other.
- A site that works and that they can update: plain Launch. Don't sell a rebuild to someone who doesn't need one.

**3. "Can you commit an hour a week for twelve weeks, on camera, working?"**

- Yes, and they mean it: either tier works.
- Hesitation, "my manager might join instead," or travel-heavy season ahead: not yet. The program is working sessions, not lessons to catch up on. Offer to talk again when the calendar clears.

**4. "Are you comfortable putting a subscription and a program fee on the business card?"**

- Yes: proceed. Their Claude subscription goes on their own card under their own name, billed by Anthropic. K&A never provides or holds their account.
- No, or they ask to run on someone else's account: full stop on that point. Everything they build has to be theirs, or the whole pitch ("you own it, it keeps running if you walk away") is false.

---

## Honest no-fit criteria

Say no, kindly and clearly, when any of these are true:

- They want to "learn AI" in general rather than fix a named problem. They churn, and they cost the same to serve.
- They're shopping for a certificate or a course. This is neither.
- They can't commit the weekly hour. No cadence, no result.
- There's genuinely nothing worth building. Say so on the call. "I'd rather tell you no than take two grand for a bad fit" is the line, and it's true, and it's also what gets the referral later.
- They want the site but refuse the program. That's a K&A website project, quoted separately. Fine outcome, different conversation.

## Payment notes

- $2,000 can split into 3 payments of $700 for businesses that need it, same as K&A does on builds.
- Launch + Site is $3,999 flat.
- No other numbers get quoted on this call. Custom build work beyond the program scope is quoted separately by Alex, never ballparked here.
