## Narration script for Amy
It runs 1,073 words, about 7:10 of speech at 150 wpm, or about 7:35 with pauses and the end screen.

**Hook:** Someone nearby searches for what you do. Your profile shows old hours, and the website button leads to a page that won't load. So they call someone else. Here's the fifteen minute fix.

**Intro:** This is K and A Performance, a web studio in Gainesville, Florida. Everything in this video comes from Google's own Business Profile help pages. The real profile you'll see is ours. The edit screens are simple illustrations, so yours may look a little different. To follow along, sign in to the Google account that manages your profile, search for your business name, and choose Edit profile. In the Google Maps app, tap Business, then Edit profile. We'll go in order of what trips customers up first.

**Fix 1:** Fix one. Your primary category. It's the one label that says what your business is, and Google says your categories affect how you show up in local results. Pick the most specific option on the list. Google's own example: choose Nail salon, not Salon. Ours is Website designer. Here's a simple test. Finish the sentence, this business is a. Not, this business has a. Then add extra categories only for other things you truly are. Google allows up to nine more, and asks you to use as few as possible. One caution. Changing a category can ask you to verify your business again, so don't change it on a whim.

**Fix 2:** Fix two. Your hours. Under Hours, check every day you're open. If you close for lunch, add two time ranges on the same day. Then open Special hours. That's where holidays go. Google suggests confirming your holiday hours even when they match your regular hours, so customers can trust them. Coming up: Veterans Day is Wednesday, November eleventh, and Thanksgiving is Thursday, November twenty sixth. Christmas Day and New Year's Day both land on a Friday. Special hours cover up to six days in a row. Closed for a week or longer? Mark the business temporarily closed instead. And if part of your business runs on different times, like delivery or pickup, use More hours.

**Fix 3:** Fix three. Your website link and your phone number. First, click your own website button. It should go to your site, and the page should load. Then a small upgrade: add tracking tags to the end of that link, like the ones on screen. They're called U T M tags. With them, your website analytics can show which visits came from your Google profile, instead of lumping them in with everything else. Now your phone number. Keep it visible. Google ended the chat feature on Business Profiles in July twenty twenty four. In some regions, customers can now text you or reach you on WhatsApp from your profile, and Google recommends a visible phone number for that.

**Fix 4:** Fix four. Your description. You get up to seven hundred fifty characters. Say what you do, who you do it for, where you work, and what makes you different. Google's rules are simple: no links, and no promotions, prices or sales. Write it the way you'd explain your business to a new neighbor. Google may offer to suggest a description for you. If you use it, read every word before you save, and fix anything that isn't true.

**Fix 5:** Fix five. Services and products. People search for the job, not your business name. If your category supports services, choose Edit services. Pick from the suggestions, like Install faucet or Repair toilet for a plumber, or add your own. Each service can have a short description and a price. Google says when someone nearby searches for a service you offer, it may be highlighted on your profile. If you sell physical products, the product editor lets you add them with a photo. List only what you really sell, and keep it current.

**Fix 6:** Fix six. Photos. Google says photos help customers decide. Add the outside, so people recognize the building. Then the inside, your products, your team, and your work. Google asks for photos that are in focus, well lit, and free of heavy filters. Use JPG or PNG, between ten kilobytes and five megabytes, and seven twenty by seven twenty pixels is the recommended size. Pick a sharp cover photo. Google notes a low quality cover can be replaced by one a customer uploads. You can add short videos too, up to thirty seconds. New photos can take a day or two to appear.

**Fix 7:** Fix seven. Reply to your reviews. You'll need a verified profile. Choose Read reviews, then Reply. Thank people for the good ones. For the hard ones, keep it short, stay specific, and apologize when it fits. Your reply shows under the review as the business, after Google checks it against its policies. That's usually quick, but it can take longer. And one rule never to break: don't offer anything in exchange for a review, or for changing or removing one.

**Fix 8:** Fix eight. Attributes. These are the small facts that answer questions before anyone has to ask. Go to Edit profile, then More. You'll see options like accessibility details, such as a wheelchair accessible entrance, and business identity, such as veteran owned or women owned. Only mark what's true. Some attributes are filled in from what customers report, and the list you see depends on your category and region.

**Fix 9:** Fix nine. If customers book appointments with you, add a booking link. From your profile, choose Bookings, then Add link, and paste the page where people actually book. Google's rules: that page has to let customers finish the booking, it has to load when Google checks it, and no link shorteners or social media pages. Your profile has to be verified first.

**Recap:** Quick recap. The most specific category. Hours, including holidays. A website link that works, with tracking tags, and a visible phone number. A clear description. Your services and products. Sharp, current photos. Replies to your reviews. True attributes. And a booking link if you take bookings. Google checks edits before they go live. Most show up in about ten minutes, but some can take up to thirty days, so look again next week.

**CTA:** Your Google profile works best when it matches your website exactly. That's the side we build at K and A Performance. The link is in the description, or call Alex at nine oh four, two one oh, one oh seven one.

**End screen:** Want another quick fix? Watch this one next, and subscribe for a new walkthrough every week.

**Removed or changed recently:**
- **Chat is gone** (July 2024, confirmed by Google). The script says so.
- **Q&A status is unclear.** "Edit your Business Profile" (answer 3039617) still says owners can answer questions in the Q&A section, "available only for select business categories and regions". But Google's developer change log says the Q&A API was discontinued on Nov 3, 2025, announced Sept 15, 2025 as "updating the Q&A functionality" (https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/qanda/change-log). Only third-party blogs say public Q&A was removed from Search and Maps starting Dec 2025; that isn't confirmed on a Google page. This is why Q&A is left out of the script.
- **Link limits conflict:** answer 13769188 says 20 links per transaction type, and answer 6218037 says up to 10. The script avoids the number.
- **UTM on the profile link is K&A's practice, not a Google recommendation.** No Business Profile Help page mentions UTM tags. The script presents it as "a small upgrade" and cites GA's UTM guidance only on how to tag.
