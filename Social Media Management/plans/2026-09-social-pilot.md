## After-trial pricing: why $600, locked

National basic social media packages run about $500 to $1,500 a month, and agency packages of 12 posts a month start near $750. $600 sits below that on purpose: this is Gainesville, and it is a new service line with no case studies yet. Pilot clients take the risk of going first, so they keep $600 for as long as they stay. Later clients will likely pay $750 once there are case studies to show.

## Operations

- Each client gets its own content folders (plan, day folders, media) and its own Post Desk view, kept apart from K&A's `To Be Released/`.
- The tools are single-brand today: Metricool brand 7076479 is written into `tools/social.mjs` and the README, and `review.mjs` reads one folder tree. They need multi-brand support (a brand id and a root folder per client) when the first client signs, before any of that client's posts are made.
