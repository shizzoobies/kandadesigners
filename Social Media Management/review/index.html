How to post a Story with a link sticker
  1. Save the day's image to your phone (Save image, below).
  2. Instagram, tap +, then Story, and pick the image.
  3. Tap the sticker icon, then Link, and paste the URL (Copy URL, below).
  4. Tap "Customize sticker text" and type the sticker text.
  5. Drag the sticker over the dashed box, then tap Your story.
  6. Come back here and tick Posted.
