Grab your phone. Open your website. You have 10 seconds.

Your customers run this test every time they find you on their phone. Run it yourself this weekend, two seconds per check:

1. It loads fast. Turn off Wi-Fi and open your site on cell data. A blank screen or a spinner at two seconds sends people back to the search results.
2. You can tap to call. Tap your phone number. If it does not start a call, you are asking a customer to copy it by hand.
3. Your hours are easy to find. From the first screen, can you tell if you are open right now? Hours in a PDF, a photo of a sign, or only on Facebook do not count.
4. You can read it in the sun. Step outside and read your first paragraph at arm's length. Light gray text and tiny type fail here.
5. The book or contact button is obvious. Without scrolling, point at the button that gets someone in touch with you.

We measured our own homepage for checks 1 and 4: the headline was on screen in 1.8 seconds (median of five cold loads on a throttled Slow 4G phone profile), and the body text measures 15.53 to 1 against its background, where WCAG asks for at least 4.5 to 1.

The takeaway: anything you could not tick is costing you customers. Fix the one you failed first, then run the test again.
