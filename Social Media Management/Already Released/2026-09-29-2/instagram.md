Your phone in the sun. Can anyone read your website?

Five contrast fixes you can make today, each measured against WCAG 2.2 from the real hex values:

1. Light gray body text: 2.52 to 1. Darken it to 7.62 to 1.
2. White text on a bright photo: 1.39 to 1. A dark scrim takes it to 5.55 to 1.
3. White text on a bright green button: 2.27 to 1. A deeper green gets 5.01 to 1.
4. Placeholder text used as the label: 1.74 to 1. Add a real label.
5. Links shown by color alone: 1.78 to 1 against the text around them. About 1 in 12 men see color differently. Underline them.

Body text needs 4.5 to 1. Large text needs 3 to 1. Save this for your next site check.
