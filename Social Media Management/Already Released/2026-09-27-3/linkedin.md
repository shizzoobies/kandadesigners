ADDIE is 50+ years old. It might be getting a G.

A framework making the rounds swaps two letters: Analysis, Design, Generation, Individualization, Evaluation. ADGIE.

What changes:
- Develop becomes Generate. AI drafts the videos, visuals, audio and quizzes. The designer conducts: reviews, tweaks, rebuilds.
- Implement becomes Individualize. AI adapts the course to each learner's gaps and pace.
- Analysis, Design and Evaluation stay led by people, with AI helping: draft learner personas, suggested structure, a faster feedback loop.

The number we liked most: in a 2025 survey of 90 learning professionals, 91% said they need to check AI-generated content themselves. Good. That's the job.

It's early. The framework hasn't been proven at scale inside real organizations yet, and it only works with good source material, trained designers, and guardrails for bias and privacy.

Our take: AI drafts, designers decide. That's how we build custom eLearning, from full courses to microlearning and job aids. The link is in the first comment.
