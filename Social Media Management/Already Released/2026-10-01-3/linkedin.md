When a first draft from an AI tool misses, the request is usually one line long, and the tool fills every gap with average. Four lines above the request close those gaps:

1. Who it is for. Name the reader and what is on their mind.
2. What good looks like. Length, tone, and the one thing the reader should do.
3. What to avoid. List what would make you delete the draft.
4. The format you want. Ask for the shape, and it arrives ready to use.

This document walks through a worked example of each line, a spring email for a fictional lawn care company, then puts all four on one fill-in-the-blank card you can save. It works in any AI tool.

The takeaway: before your next request, write one sentence for each of the four lines. The reader and the limits matter most, because the tool cannot guess them.
