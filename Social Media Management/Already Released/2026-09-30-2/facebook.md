If everyone passes your quiz, it measured nothing.

A quiz that everyone aces tells you people can read, not whether they can do the job. Swipe for five fixes we use on every course, each with a weak and a strong example question:

1. Ask what they would do, not what they can recite.
2. Make every wrong answer tempting to someone.
3. Retire "All of the above."
4. Skip the trick negatives.
5. Write feedback that teaches the why.

The example questions are ours, written for a customer service course.
