Someone asks for a course. Before anyone builds one, ask five questions about the task.

A course asks people to remember something until the day they need it. A job aid only has to be there on that day. Sometimes the answer is neither, and more training would not change a thing.

The test, one question per page in this document:

1. How often do they do it? Rarely points to a job aid. Every day points to a course.
2. Is it done at a desk, or on the job with no time to look? A moment to check points to a job aid. No pause points to a course.
3. Does it change often? A job aid, so you update one page instead of retraining everyone.
4. Is the risk of a mistake high? With time to check, a checklist. With no time to check, practice first.
5. Do people already know the why? Yes points to a job aid. No points to a course.

If people know how and why and still don't do it, it is neither. Look at the process, the tool, or the incentive.

The takeaway: run the next training request through these five before it becomes a project. The last page is a card to keep.
