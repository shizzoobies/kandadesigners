Update these 5 things on your Google Business Profile this month. Save this for your next check.

1. Primary category. Pick the most specific one on Google's list. Google's own example: "Nail salon," not "Salon."
2. Hours, and holiday hours. Google suggests confirming your hours for holidays even when they match your regular ones. Veterans Day is Nov 11 and Thanksgiving is Nov 26, so set them now.
3. Services. When a local searches for something you offer, that service may be highlighted on your profile.
4. Fresh photos. The outside, the inside, your products, your team, your work. In focus, well lit, no heavy filters.
5. Review replies. Short, helpful, prompt, and an apology when it fits. Never trade anything for a review.

Every tip is paraphrased from Google's own Business Profile help pages. The category, hours and listing on the slides are our own profile, captured as Google shows it; the rest are drawn examples.
