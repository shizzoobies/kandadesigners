Before you sign with a web designer, ask these 6 questions.

A website is a real investment for a small business, and two quotes that look alike can cover very different work. These six questions show what you are actually buying:

1. Who owns the site and the domain when we are done?
2. What exactly does the price include?
3. What will I pay every month after launch?
4. Will it load fast on a phone?
5. Will it show up when locals search?
6. After launch, who makes changes, and what do they cost?

Each page of this document says why the question matters for your business, and how we answer it, taken straight from our own site.

The takeaway: ask all six of anyone you are considering, and get every answer in writing. A low quote that leaves out the writing, the speed work, or the search setup costs more later.
