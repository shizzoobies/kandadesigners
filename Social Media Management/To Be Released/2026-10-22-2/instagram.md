From review to FAQ answer in 4 steps. The AI drafts. You decide. Save this recipe.

Ingredients: your public reviews, your own facts (hours, prices, parking, policies), an AI tool, one prompt, and you.

1. Collect your reviews. Copy your public reviews into one document, newest first. Private messages and anything told in confidence stay out.
2. Pull the questions. Paste the reviews and ask which questions they answer for a new customer.
3. Draft each answer in your voice. Give the AI one question and your own facts, and tell it to use nothing else.
4. Check every claim. This is the step you don't skip. Match each sentence in the draft to a fact on your list. Can't point to the fact? Cut the sentence.

To serve: publish each answer on the page where the question comes up. A price question goes on your prices page, parking on your contact page.

The AI drafts. You decide what goes on your website.
