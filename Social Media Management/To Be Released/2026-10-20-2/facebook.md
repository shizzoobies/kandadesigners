1. A phone number that dials on tap. Make it a tel: link, so a visitor on a phone taps once instead of copying ten digits.
2. An address that opens the map. Link it to your map listing so one tap starts directions. No storefront? Name the towns you serve and link your Google profile.
3. Hours, or when you pick up. "Weekdays 9 to 5" or "By appointment" both count.
4. One short form. Name, email or phone, and the message. Ask the rest in your reply.
5. What happens after they send. Who replies, and roughly when. Say it on the page, then again in the thank-you message.
