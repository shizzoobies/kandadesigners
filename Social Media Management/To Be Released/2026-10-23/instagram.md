What we check before a website goes live: 7 checks, shown on a site we built.

Before a site launches, it gets the same pass. Here it is on Thrillers Mobile VR (thrillersvr.com), captured Sept 30:

1. Tab through it. All 26 stops on the home page come in order, each with a focus ring you can see. The first press lands on "Skip to content."
2. The 10-second phone test. On the first phone screen, a new visitor can say what it is (a mobile VR trailer) and where (Middleburg, FL).
3. Contrast. Text colors measured, not eyeballed. WCAG asks for 4.5:1; the lowest pair we found was 7.55:1.
4. Every link returns 200. Every page on the site, and every link on each page.
5. The form sends. Labels, required marks and a clear error, then one test send before launch. Here we only show the field states; nothing was sent.
6. The 404 page. A wrong address gets a real 404 and a way back.
7. Speed on a phone. More on that soon.
