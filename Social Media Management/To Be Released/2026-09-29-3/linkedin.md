Your phone in the sun. Can anyone read your website?

Contrast is easy to miss on an office monitor and hard to miss outdoors on a phone. WCAG 2.2 sets the line: 4.5 to 1 for body text, 3 to 1 for large text. Every ratio in this document is computed from the real hex values.

Five fixes you can make today:

1. Light gray body text measures 2.52 to 1. A darker gray gets 7.62 to 1.
2. White text on a bright photo measures 1.39 to 1. A dark scrim behind it gets 5.55 to 1.
3. White text on a bright green button measures 2.27 to 1. A deeper green gets 5.01 to 1.
4. Placeholder text used as the label measures 1.74 to 1. Put a real label above the field.
5. Links shown by color alone measure 1.78 to 1 against the text around them. About 1 in 12 men see color differently. Underline your links.

The takeaway: run your three most used text and background pairs through a contrast checker and fix anything under 4.5 to 1. Start with buttons and form labels, where a customer is trying to act.
