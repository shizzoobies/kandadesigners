Wrong answer? Say why, not just "incorrect." 5 rules for feedback that teaches. Save this for the next quiz you write.

1. Name what went wrong. Not just that it is wrong, but which part. Our RFI course says "Needs another pass: References, Proposal."
2. Point at the rule. Send them back to the idea that decides the answer. Our strength course says "Notice whether the task lowers and rises, moves mainly at the hips, pushes, pulls or travels with a load."
3. Show the right move. When the method is the lesson, show the working. Our P&L course walks through the subtraction that gets to $1,000.
4. Let them retry. Mark the miss, leave it open, and ask for another go. Our safety course says "Revisit this placement. Read the situation and try a different control."
5. Keep it short. What to try, then why. Our nutrition course: "Try another part of the plate. Brown rice is a whole grain. It retains its bran and germ."
