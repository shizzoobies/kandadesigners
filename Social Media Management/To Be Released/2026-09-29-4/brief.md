## What the viewer gets
Action Mapping in 30 seconds: Cathy Moore's model designs training around what people need to do, not everything they could know. Four steps in order: the business goal, the actions, practice, and only then the knowledge that's truly needed. A realistic practice scenario shows consequences instead of "wrong", and a detour reminds designers that sometimes the answer isn't a course at all. It lands on K&A's custom eLearning.

## Hook
"Most courses start in the wrong place." A typical route: "Everything they should know" to "Quiz" to a dead end.
