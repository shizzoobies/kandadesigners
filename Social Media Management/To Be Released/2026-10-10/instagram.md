Stock photos make you look like everyone else. 5 photo rules for a small business website. Save this for your next photo day.

1. Use your own photos, not stock. A photo of your real shop, your work or your team shows visitors who they'd be dealing with. Put one on the first screen.
2. Show the product, the place or the people. A photo that shows none of the three is only decoration.
3. Size it for the space it fills. Resize to about twice the width it shows at, then save it as WebP or a compressed JPG. A photo straight off one of our phones is 4,032 px wide and 3.2 MB. Our team portraits on our site are 400 px and 34 KB.
4. Write alt text that says what's in it. One plain sentence: what's in the photo, and why it's there.
5. Keep words out of the picture. Put them on the page, next to or over the photo.
