A course asks people to remember a step for weeks until the day it comes up. A job aid only has to be there on that day, right next to the work.

Both screens in this reel are real, from our sample courses on our site. The RFI course ends with a four-point "Before you send" checklist to keep beside the RFI log. The safety course has each learner build a walk-through card to carry on the site walk.

A good rule of thumb: if the task is rare, the steps are the same every time, and there is a moment to look something up, hand them a job aid. Save the course for judgment people need to practice.
