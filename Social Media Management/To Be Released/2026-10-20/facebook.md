A contact form has one job: get the first message to you. Keep three fields:
1. Name.
2. Email or phone, whichever you reply with.
3. The message.

Address, budget, company, how they found you: all of it can wait for your reply, once they've already written.

Our own contact page asks for exactly three: name, email, message.
