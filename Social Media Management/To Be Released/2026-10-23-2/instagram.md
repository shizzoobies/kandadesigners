What we check before a website goes live, one inspection tag per check. Shown on a site we built. Save this for your next launch.

Our pre-launch pass, run on Thrillers Mobile VR (thrillersvr.com) on Sept 30:

1. Tab through it. All 26 stops on the home page in order, each with a visible focus ring.
2. The 10-second phone test. The first phone screen says what it is and where.
3. Contrast. Measured against WCAG: 4.5:1 is the minimum, 7.55:1 was the lowest we found.
4. Every link returns 200. 6 pages, 6 returned 200.
5. The form sends. Labels, required marks, a clear error, and one test send before launch. Nothing was sent for this post.
6. The 404 page. A real 404 status and two ways back.
7. Speed on a phone. More on that soon.
