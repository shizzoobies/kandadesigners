Above the fold: 4 things a visitor needs in 5 seconds. We marked up our own homepage on a phone, and all four fit on the first screen.

On an iPhone 13 in Safari, the first screen of a site is 390 by 664 pixels. Before anyone scrolls, it has to answer four questions:

1. What do you do?
2. Where?
3. Why should I trust you?
4. What do I tap next?

Our homepage, measured: what we do is in the line under the headline. "We work out of Gainesville, Florida" is right there. Five stars and "5.0 on Google, 8 reviews" sit at 566 pixels. "Call" is in the header, and "Start a project" ends at 660 pixels, just inside the fold.

The takeaway: run the 5-second test on your own homepage. Open it on your phone, look for 5 seconds, then look away. If a stranger could not name all four, fix the first screen before you touch anything else. One line of proof, a rating with its count, is often enough. Then check it at a real phone height, not just a desktop window.
