What does a contact page owe a visitor? Five things. We checked each one on our own page, and we didn't get all five.

1. A phone number that dials on tap. Make it a tel: link, so a visitor on a phone taps once instead of copying ten digits. Ours: yes.

2. An address that opens the map. Link it to your map listing so one tap starts directions. If you serve customers at their place, name the towns you cover and link your Google profile. Ours: not yet. We list our towns, but there is no map link.

3. Hours, or when you pick up. "Weekdays 9 to 5" or "By appointment" both count. Ours: not yet. The page says messages get a reply within 24 hours, but not when the phone gets answered.

4. One short form. Name, email or phone, and the message. Ask the rest in your reply. Ours: yes, three fields.

5. What happens after they send. Who replies, and roughly when, on the page and again in the thank-you message. Ours: yes. "We read every word and reply within 24 hours," from Alex and Kristina.

Three of five, checked on Sept 30. We're fixing the other two too.

A contact page is where a visit turns into a conversation, so it's one of the first pages we check when we build or rework a small business website. The link is in the first comment.
