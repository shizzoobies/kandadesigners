Set a page in seven fonts and every line competes for attention. Nothing leads, so nothing gets read first.

The fix:
1. Pick one font for headings.
2. Pick one for body text, the one that has to stay easy to read at small sizes.
3. Maybe one for labels, like kickers, hours or tags.
4. For everything else, change the size or the weight, not the font.

Our own site runs on three: Schibsted Grotesk for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for body text and Lenia Mono for labels. Your logo is artwork, so it doesn't count.
