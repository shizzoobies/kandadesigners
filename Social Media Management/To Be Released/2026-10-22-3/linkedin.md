Your customer reviews already hold the questions your website should answer. Here is a four-step recipe for turning them into FAQ answers with AI, without letting the AI decide what you publish.

Ingredients: your public reviews, your own facts (hours, prices, parking, policies), an AI tool, one prompt, and you.

1. Collect your reviews. Copy your public reviews into one document, newest first. Private messages and anything told in confidence stay out.

2. Pull the questions. Paste the reviews and ask which questions they answer for someone deciding whether to buy. In our run on six sample reviews, it returned 13, each with the review words that answer it.

3. Draft each answer in your voice. Give the AI one question and your own facts, and tell it to use nothing else.

4. Check every claim. This is the step that matters. Match each sentence in the draft to a fact on your list. If you can't point to the fact, cut the sentence. Our sample draft passed: five claims, five matching facts, and it left out a fact that didn't answer the question.

To serve: publish each answer on the page where the question comes up, not only on an FAQ page.

The AI drafts. The owner decides.
