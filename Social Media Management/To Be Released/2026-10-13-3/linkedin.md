A button only works if people click it. Here are five rules for buttons, each checked on our own site with the real numbers.

1. The label says what happens. "Book a table" beats "Submit". A visitor should know what a tap does before they tap it.
2. One main button per screen. Give the action you want most the strongest look and make the rest quieter. On a phone, our home page's first screen shows one filled button and one outline.
3. Big enough to tap. Aim for at least 44 by 44 pixels (WCAG 2.5.5; 2.5.8 sets a 24 pixel minimum). Our Start a project button measures 148.7 by 47 on a phone.
4. It passes contrast. The label needs at least 4.5:1 against the button color (WCAG 1.4.3). White on amber is 3.19:1 and fails. Ink on amber, our label, is 5.29:1. Compute it rather than eyeball it.
5. You can reach it by keyboard. Press Tab: on our home page a rust focus outline lands on Start a project after 5 presses, at 6.73:1 against the page.
