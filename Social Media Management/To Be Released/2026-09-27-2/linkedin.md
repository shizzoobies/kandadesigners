Sunday night. 10 minutes. A week that doesn't run you.

If your Monday starts with a pile instead of a plan, here are three prompts to paste into any AI tool before the week starts. Fill in the brackets and each one hands you something you can use:

1. Sort the week. Your messy to-do list becomes a Monday to Friday plan, three priorities a day at most, hardest one first, with a list of what can wait.
2. Send the three. Short drafts of the messages you keep putting off, one clear ask each, with every guess marked. The messages we avoid are often the ones tied to money: quotes, invoices, follow-ups.
3. Drop one thing. The one commitment to say no to, move, or hand off this week, the reason in two sentences, and a polite message to say it.

The full prompts are in this document, with all three on one page to save.

The takeaway: the AI drafts, and you decide. It drafts the plan, the messages, and the one thing to drop. You decide what matters most, what goes out, and whether you drop it.
