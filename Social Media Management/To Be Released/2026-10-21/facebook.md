Show it, then let them try it. A simple pattern for teaching any skill.
Training built around practice: https://ka-performancefl.com/training/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=2026-10-21

1. Watch one done. Show a finished example, and point out why each part is there.
2. Do one with hints. Let them build one with the pieces and the labels in front of them.
3. Do one alone. Give them a new problem, with no pieces and no labels.
