Some of your customers never touch a mouse. Five keyboard checks:

1. Tab reaches every link and button.
2. You can always see where you are: a visible outline on whatever Tab just reached.
3. The menu opens from the keyboard.
4. Forms submit with Enter.
5. Nothing traps you: Esc or Tab always gets you out of a pop-up.

Swipe for each check on our own site, keyboard only, with what failing looks like and why it costs you customers. Our site passes all five.
