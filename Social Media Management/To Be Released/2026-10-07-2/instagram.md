Course, job aid, or neither? Five questions decide it. Save this for the next training request.

Before anyone builds a course, ask these about the task:

1. How often do they do it? Rarely points to a job aid. Every day points to a course.
2. Is it done at a desk, or on the job with no time to look? A moment to check points to a job aid. No pause points to a course.
3. Does it change often? Yes points to a job aid, so you update one page instead of retraining everyone.
4. Is the risk of a mistake high? If they can check as they go, a checklist. If there is no time to check, practice first.
5. Do people already know the why? Yes points to a job aid. No points to a course.

Count the answers. The side with more wins. And if people know how and why and still don't do it, it's neither: fix the process, the tool, or the incentive.
