Your business name, phone and address should read exactly the same in five places. We checked our own on Sept 28 and found one that didn't.

Start with one master record, written one way. Then copy it into every place customers look you up:

1. Your website. Put your name, phone and city in the footer, so every page carries them.
2. Your Google Business Profile. If you don't serve customers at your address, Google says to remove it and set a service area.
3. Your Facebook Page. Ours showed the name and website but no phone and no city to signed-out visitors. Two fields in the Page's contact info fix it.
4. Apple Maps. Claim your place card in Apple Business Connect and copy the same details.
5. Bing Places. It can import your Google profile, so the two start out matching. Recheck it after any change.

Why it matters: Google's own help page says businesses with complete and accurate info are more likely to show up in local search results. And an old number on one listing is a call that never reaches you.
