A good Google review can do more work after you say thanks. Five things to do with it:

1. Reply in public. Thank them by name and mention what they mentioned. The next customer reads your reply too, so it is part of your first impression.

2. Answer the question it raised, on your site. When a review says "I didn't know you did that," other people didn't know either. Put the answer on the page where they look first.

3. Quote it on your site. Ask first. Then use their words exactly, with their permission, on the page the review is about.

4. Fix what it flagged. A fair complaint is free advice. Fix it, then say so in your reply, where the next customer will see it.

5. Ask the next happy customer. Google gives every business profile a review link and a QR code. Share it when someone says thanks in person, and offer nothing in return: Google doesn't allow incentives for reviews.
