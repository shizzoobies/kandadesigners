Keep a "best replies" file: the AI-assisted customer replies you were proud to send, saved so the next draft can start from them.

Five steps:

1. Save it. When an AI-assisted reply came out right after your edits, file the version you actually sent. Start with the questions that come in every week: pickup times, quotes, hours.

2. Clean it. Before a reply goes in the file, swap names, dates and order details for blanks. The file keeps your wording, not a customer's private details.

3. Reuse it. When the same question comes in, paste the saved reply with your usual prompt and the new email, plus one line: "Here is a reply I sent before and liked. Match its tone, length and sign-off."

4. Check it. A good old reply can carry an old price. Before you reuse one, check names, dates, prices and policies against how things are today, and fix the file when something changes.

5. Send it. For a routine question, the saved reply may already be enough. Fill in the blanks, read it once, and send. Not every reply needs a new draft.
