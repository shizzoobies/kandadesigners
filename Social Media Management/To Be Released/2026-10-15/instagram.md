Before you paste a customer email into AI, take these 4 things out.

1. Card numbers. No reply needs them.
2. Passwords and door codes. Anything that opens a door or an account.
3. Health details. Keep the request, drop the reason: "Please skip the upstairs" stays in, the surgery comes out.
4. Anything told in confidence. If they asked you to keep it quiet, keep it out.

Then check your AI tool's data settings, so you know how your chats are stored and used. Settings differ from tool to tool, so look at the one you use, and at your plan's terms if you use it for work.
