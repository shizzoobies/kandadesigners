5 things to do with a Google review after you say thanks. Save this for the next one.

1. Reply in public. Thank them by name and mention what they mentioned. The next customer reads your reply too.
2. Answer its question on your site. If a review says "didn't know you did that," others didn't know either. Put the answer where they look first.
3. Quote it on your site. Ask first. Then use their words exactly, on the page it's about.
4. Fix what it flagged. A fair complaint is free advice. Fix it, then say so in your reply.
5. Ask the next happy customer. Share your Google review link or QR code. Offer nothing in return: Google doesn't allow incentives for reviews.

This week: pick one review and do one of the five.
