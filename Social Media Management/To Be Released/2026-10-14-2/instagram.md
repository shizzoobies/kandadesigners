One module, one objective. Here's how to split a long course into short modules that each teach one thing. Save this for your next course.

1. Start from the job, not the chapter list. Write down what people must be able to do after the training. Each action can become a module.
2. Give each module one objective, written as one sentence. If the sentence needs an "and", it's two modules.
3. Make every screen serve that objective. Anything that doesn't goes to a job aid or another module.
4. Let learners do the thing before the module ends, not just read about it.

Then put the modules in the order the work happens.

The example is our RFI sample course. Its one objective is on the first screen: "Turn a drawing conflict into a request the design team can act on." Every step points at that one request, and the module ends with the learner building one. Those screens are real captures from our site; the chapter list and the three-module split are drawn examples.
