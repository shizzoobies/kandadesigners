Training everyone can take: 3 accessibility checks for any course.
Training built for every learner: https://ka-performancefl.com/training/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=2026-10-14

1. Captions on every video, and a transcript for all audio. Try it: turn the sound off. Can you still follow?
2. Text you can read. Try it: measure the contrast. Body text needs at least 4.5:1 to meet WCAG AA. The body text on the cover of our strength sample course measures 10.3:1.
3. It works with a keyboard. Try it: put the mouse away and press Tab. Can you see where you are, and can you finish with Tab and Enter?

Every screen in this reel is a real capture from our sample courses: the audio introduction in our RFI course with its transcript, the cover of our strength course, and the hazard hunt in our safety course, done with the keyboard only.

Accessibility is part of how we build training. Client delivery targets WCAG 2.1 AA, with checks in the environment your learners use.
