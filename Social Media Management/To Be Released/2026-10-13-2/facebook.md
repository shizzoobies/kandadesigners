Buttons that get clicked: five rules, each one checked on our own site.
Check your buttons: https://ka-performancefl.com/services/accessibility/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=2026-10-13-2

1. The label says what happens. "Book a table" beats "Submit". A visitor should know what a tap does before they tap it.
2. One main button per screen. Give the action you want most the strongest look and make the rest quieter. On a phone, our home page's first screen shows one filled button and one outline.
3. Big enough to tap. Aim for at least 44 by 44 pixels. Our Start a project button measures 148.7 by 47 on a phone.
4. It passes contrast. The label needs at least 4.5:1 against the button color. White on amber is 3.19:1 and fails. Ink on amber, our label, is 5.29:1.
5. You can reach it by keyboard. Press Tab: on our home page a rust outline lands on Start a project after 5 presses.
