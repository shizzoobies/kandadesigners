What we check before a website goes live, shown on a site we built and already launched: Thrillers Mobile VR (thrillersvr.com).

Seven checks, captured on the live site on Sept 30:

1. Tab through it. Every stop on the home page, in order, with a focus ring you can see. Thrillers has 26, and the first one is "Skip to content."

2. The 10-second phone test. On the first phone screen, with no scrolling, a new visitor can say what the business is and where it is.

3. Contrast. Text colors measured against WCAG, not eyeballed. The minimum is 4.5:1; the lowest pair on Thrillers is 7.55:1.

4. Every link returns 200. Every page on the site, and every link on each page. Six pages, six 200s.

5. The form sends. Labels, required marks, and an error that says what to fix, then one test send before launch. For this document we only captured the field states; nothing was sent through the client's form.

6. The 404 page. A wrong address should return a real 404 status and give people a way back, not a blank page.

7. Speed on a phone. More on that soon.
