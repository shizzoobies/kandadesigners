Update these 5 things on your Google Business Profile this month.

For a lot of local customers, the profile is the first thing they see, before the website. Every point in this document is paraphrased from Google's own Business Profile help pages, with the source on each slide.

1. Primary category. Pick the most specific one on the list. Google's own example: "Nail salon," not "Salon."
2. Hours, including holiday hours. Google suggests confirming holiday hours even when they match your regular ones. Veterans Day is Nov 11 and Thanksgiving is Nov 26.
3. Services. When someone nearby searches for something you offer, that service may be highlighted on your profile.
4. Fresh photos. The outside, the inside, your team, your work. In focus, well lit, no heavy filters.
5. Review replies. Short, helpful, prompt, with an apology when one is owed. Never trade anything for a review.

We checked our own profile against the list too. The category, hours and listing on the slides are ours, captured as Google shows them; the rest are drawn examples.
