Where should a small business start with AI? With the jobs where a mistake is easy to catch.

Three that fit, and the part of each one to keep for yourself:

1. First drafts. Customer replies, social posts, product descriptions, the first version of a page. Keep the facts, any promise about when or what, and the final send. A draft can sound right and still get a price or a date wrong.

2. Summaries. A long email thread, call notes, a month of reviews grouped by theme. Keep the decision. A summary tells you what was said, not what to do, and anything about money, contracts, or health gets read in the original.

3. Sorting and tagging. Inbox triage, tagging customer questions by topic, labeling receipts by category. Keep the categories, a weekly spot check, and every upset customer.

Why these three first: they come back every week, the work can be checked at a glance, and a miss stays on your desk instead of reaching a customer.

The takeaway: hand AI the first pass and keep the last word. This document has a save card with all three on one page.
