What Direct Primary Care means here.
Link in bio for familypracticedirect.com.

Direct Primary Care is a membership: one flat monthly fee, month to month, no contract.
Members get longer visits, same or next day access, and can call or text their provider.
It sits beside the other ways to be seen here: we accept insurance, and we see patients who do not have any.
