Your annual physical: what to expect.
Link in bio for familypracticedirect.com.

What we go over: your health history and medications, the screenings and vaccinations you may be due for, any ongoing condition like blood pressure or cholesterol, and the questions you have been saving.
What to bring: a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of your medications.
Why once a year: it keeps your history current and gives you a set time to talk with your provider. How often you need a checkup depends on your age and health, so ask your provider.
We accept insurance, see uninsured patients, and offer Direct Primary Care memberships.

General information, not medical advice. Call the office with questions, or 911 in an emergency.
