- **It is Justin's funnel, on his dates.** His Sept 18 timeline puts the Founders funnel at
  Oct 2 to 15 and the campaign launch with public prices on Oct 15. His Sept 28 brief sets six
  phases: something new is coming, our story, the experience, what it means to be a Founder,
  the reveal, sales. The site holds prices until Oct 15. These two weeks walk the phases in
  order and arrive at the reveal the day the site flips.
- **One ask the whole way.** The splash page has one action, Join the Founders List. Until the
  Founders pages are public, every post sends people there, and nothing says "buy".
- **The tiers appear by name before they appear by price.** Phase 4 shows each tier's hours and
  spots, which the brief already fixes. Prices wait for Thu Oct 15 and Justin's confirmation.
- **No venue photos exist.** Real footage is Hannah's two walkthroughs of the empty space.
  Everything else is typographic brand cards. No AI imagery.
- **The Founders Cup waits.** The game is on hold, has no dates, and still needs counsel. A
  post that says it is live would break the brand guide's rule. Two Cup posts are briefed now
  and built the day Justin gives a ship date.
