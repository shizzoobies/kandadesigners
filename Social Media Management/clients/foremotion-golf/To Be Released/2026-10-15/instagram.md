What a Founding Membership is.
Link in bio to join the Founders List.

A Founding Membership is a limited prepaid membership offered before Fore Motion Golf officially opens.
It includes simulator hours, membership benefits, early access, Founder recognition and priority opportunities during the membership term.
There are four: Founding Practice, Founding Club, Founding Tour and Founding Corporate Partner.
The Founders List gets first access.
Limited availability. Benefits and terms governed by the Founding Membership Agreement.
