Welcome to Claude Code

This short course gets you comfortable with Claude Code, the AI pair programmer that works in your terminal. You will type the real commands and see realistic output in a safe sandbox, then walk away ready to use the real thing.

A safe sandbox. You do not have a license yet, and you do not need one here. Nothing installs on your machine, no account is charged, and you cannot break anything. Just follow along and build the muscle memory.
