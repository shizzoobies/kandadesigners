PHOTOGRAPHY SLOTS
Heat, done well (training-samples/sauna)

Every photo below is decorative. All meaning in the module is carried by
the text, so a missing file costs nothing but the picture: each slot is a
CSS background-image sitting on top of a pale cedar panel with a drawn
plank texture under it, so an absent file renders as a finished wooden
plate rather than a broken image icon. Drop the licensed files in under
exactly these names and they appear with no code change.

  hero.jpg
    Screen 1 backdrop, behind a heavy steam-white wash.
    Landscape, 1600px wide minimum, roughly 16:9.
    The inside of a cedar sauna: benches, a stove, a pile of stones,
    warm low light. Nothing legible in frame: no signage, no brand, no
    face close enough to identify. It sits under a 90 to 96 percent
    light wash, so choose an image that reads as warmth and shape
    rather than detail, and avoid one with a bright window in it.

  stones.jpg
    Screen 2 side panel, under the heat dial.
    Square, 900px on a side minimum.
    Sauna stones on a stove, with a wooden ladle resting on or beside
    them. Close in, warm, a little steam is welcome.

  window.jpg
    Screen 3 side panel.
    Portrait, 900px on the short side minimum.
    Light coming through a small sauna window, or a plain cedar wall
    detail with the grain and the bench edge visible. Quiet, no people.

  lake.jpg
    Screen 4 side panel, below the breathing pacer.
    Portrait, 900px on the short side minimum.
    The cool-down: a cold lake, a snowy dock, a plunge pool, or a door
    open onto cold air. Cool colour, to sit against the warm plates
    everywhere else.

  towel.jpg
    Screen 6 side panel, below the list of cautions.
    Square, 900px on a side minimum.
    A folded towel and a water bottle on a cedar bench. Plain glass or
    plain bottle: no readable label, no brand mark.

All plates crop with background-size: cover and centre themselves, so
keep the subject near the middle of the frame. Nothing is ever placed on
top of a photo except on screen 1, where the wash was measured against a
pure black pixel as the worst case: even then the ground stays light
enough for the body copy, the heading and the small print to clear
4.5:1. If you swap that wash, measure it again before shipping.

Licensing note: these are placeholders for licensed stock or owned
photography. No client site, no employer material, no real venue that
can be identified, and no identifiable person without a release.
