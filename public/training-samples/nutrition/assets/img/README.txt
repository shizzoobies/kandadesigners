PHOTOGRAPHY SLOTS
Build the plate, skip the diet (training-samples/nutrition)

Every photo below is decorative. All the meaning in this module is carried by
the text and by the drawn plate, so a missing file costs nothing but the
picture: each slot is a CSS background-image sitting on a kraft panel with a
linen tooth over it and a crate slat closing the top and bottom edge, so an
absent file renders as a finished textured plate rather than a broken image
icon. Drop the licensed files in under exactly these names and they appear
with no code change.

  hero.jpg
    Card 1 backdrop (the cover), behind a heavy cream wash.
    Landscape, 1600px wide minimum, roughly 16:9.
    Produce on a wooden kitchen counter. Nothing legible: no packaging with a
    readable brand, no signage, no face close enough to identify. It sits
    under a 90 to 95 percent cream wash with the index-card ruling over it, so
    choose an image that reads as shape and colour rather than detail.

  plate.jpg
    Card 9 side panel (your card).
    Square, 900px on the side minimum.
    A plated meal shot from directly above. Ideally a plate that already looks
    like the lesson: a good share of vegetables, a piece of protein, a starch.
    Nothing branded, no hands, no restaurant markings.

  market.jpg
    Card 6 upper side panel (protein and fibre).
    Portrait, 900px on the short side minimum.
    A market crate or a basket of vegetables. Loose produce, wooden slats,
    daylight. No stall signage, no price cards, no faces.

  pantry.jpg
    Card 6 lower side panel (protein and fibre).
    Square, 900px on the side minimum.
    Jars and grains on a shelf: beans, lentils, oats, rice. Plain jars with no
    printed labels, or labels turned away from the camera.

  label.jpg
    Card 5 side panel (reading a nutrition label), shown at 1088px and wider.
    Portrait, 900px on the short side minimum.
    Someone holding a packaged food in a shop, turning it to look at the back.
    The packet must be unreadable: no brand, no legible label text, no product
    that identifies a real company. Framing on the hands and the packet, not
    on the face.

All panels crop with background-size: cover and centre themselves, so keep the
subject near the middle of the frame. The slat bands at the top and bottom of
each panel sit over the photograph by design, so leave a little room there.

Licensing note: these are placeholders for licensed stock or owned
photography. No client material, no employer material, no real brand or
packaged product that can be identified, and no identifiable person without a
release. Nothing here should read as an endorsement of a particular food
product, because the module deliberately does not endorse one.
