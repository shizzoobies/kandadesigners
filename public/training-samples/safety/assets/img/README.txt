PHOTOGRAPHY SLOTS
Spot it before it hurts someone (training-samples/safety)

Every photo below is decorative. All meaning in the module is carried by the
text, so a missing file costs nothing but the picture: each slot is a CSS
background-image sitting on top of a charcoal panel and a faint diagonal
hazard-stripe texture, so an absent file renders as a finished textured plate
rather than a broken image icon. Drop the licensed files in under exactly these
names and they appear with no code change.

  hero.jpg
    Screen 1, the cover: the plate the title sits on, under a heavy
    charcoal overlay.
    Landscape, 1600px wide minimum, roughly 16:9.
    Wide site view. Nothing legible: no signage, no plate, no logo, no face
    close enough to identify. It sits under an 86 to 94 percent dark wash, so
    choose an image that reads as shape and silhouette rather than detail.

  scaffold.jpg
    Screen 4 side panel (access and egress).
    Portrait or square, 900px on the short side minimum.
    Access: a scaffold, a stair tower, a ladder run, a gate.

  electrical.jpg
    Screen 5 side panel (work at height and energy).
    Portrait or square, 900px on the short side minimum.
    Temporary power, a panel, cord management, a lockout point.

  housekeeping.jpg
    Screen 6 upper side panel (housekeeping).
    Portrait or square, 900px on the short side minimum.
    Staged material, swept walkway, bins at the work area.

  ppe.jpg
    Screen 6 lower side panel (protective equipment).
    Portrait or square, 900px on the short side minimum.
    Head, eye, hand or hearing protection in use.

All panels crop with background-size: cover and centre themselves, so keep the
subject near the middle of the frame.

Licensing note: these are placeholders for licensed stock or owned photography.
No client site, no employer material, and no identifiable person without a
release.
