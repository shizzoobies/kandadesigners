PHOTOGRAPHY SLOTS
Strong is a skill (training-samples/strength)

Every photo below is decorative. All meaning in the course is carried by the
text, so a missing file costs nothing but the picture: each slot is a CSS
background-image sitting on a near black panel with a faint diamond scored
into it, so an absent file renders as a finished textured plate rather than a
broken image icon. Drop the licensed files in under exactly these names and
they appear with no code change.

  hero.jpg
    Screen 1 backdrop, behind a heavy near black overlay.
    Landscape, 1600px wide minimum, roughly 16:9.
    An empty gym floor or a bare rack, early and moody, nobody in it. It sits
    under an 86 to 94 percent dark wash, so choose an image that reads as
    shape and silhouette rather than detail. Nothing legible: no gym name, no
    signage, no logo on a plate, no face close enough to identify.

  chalk.jpg
    Screen 2 upper side plate (why it matters at any age).
    Portrait, 900px on the short side minimum.
    Chalked hands, or a chalk bowl on the corner of a platform. No
    identifiable face. Hands are fine, faces are not.

  floor.jpg
    Screen 2 lower side plate (why it matters at any age).
    Square, 900px minimum.
    Rubber floor with a kettlebell sitting on it, shot from above or close to
    the ground. Texture is the point.

  rack.jpg
    Screen 3 side plate (the five movement patterns).
    Portrait, 900px on the short side minimum.
    A loaded or empty barbell sitting in a rack. No faces, no brand marks on
    the plates or the upright.

  dumbbells.jpg
    Screen 5 side plate (how hard is hard enough).
    Square, 900px minimum.
    Dumbbells racked in a row, or a single pair set down on the floor.

All plates crop with background-size: cover and centre themselves, so keep
the subject near the middle of the frame. Each one also carries a 5px steel
rail down its left edge, which lands over the leftmost few pixels of the
photograph: nothing important should sit hard against the left side.

Licensing note: these are placeholders for licensed stock or owned
photography. No client site, no employer material, no gym that can be
identified, and no identifiable person without a release.
