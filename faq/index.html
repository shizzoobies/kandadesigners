FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything we're asked most — process, pricing, timelines, and what it's like to work with us. Don't see your question? Just ask.

How does a project usually start? +

It starts with a conversation. You tell us what you're building and what's not working today; we ask questions until the goal is clear. From there we put together a scope and a custom quote — no forms, no automated estimator, just a straight answer based on what the work actually involves.

What's the typical timeline for a website? +

Most custom builds land between four and eight weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope, content readiness, and how many rounds of feedback we go through. AI integrations and brand systems can run alongside the build or extend the timeline slightly — we'll map out real dates before anything starts.

How does pricing work? Do you have a price list? +

We don't publish fixed prices, because no two projects are the same — a five-page brand site and a custom AI-powered platform aren't priced on the same scale. Every engagement gets a custom quote built around your actual scope, timeline, and goals. Reach out and we'll give you real numbers, quickly.

What's included in a website build? +

A custom design built for your brand, a fully responsive build across devices, performance and Core Web Vitals tuning, on-page SEO fundamentals, and basic analytics setup. Anything beyond that — e-commerce, custom functionality, AI features — gets scoped in up front so there are no surprises.

Do you actually build AI integrations, or is that just marketing? +

We build them. Claude API-powered assistants and natural-language features wired directly into your site, workflow automations that remove busywork, and AI audio and voice work with ElevenLabs. These are real integrations that run in production, not one-off demos — see the Services page for specifics.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance, support, and hosting? +

Yes. We offer ongoing support plans that cover updates, monitoring, and small changes after launch, plus guidance on hosting so your site stays fast and secure. If you'd rather manage hosting yourself, we'll hand off a clean setup with documentation.

Can you redesign or rebuild an existing site? +

Absolutely — it's some of our favorite work. We'll audit what's there, keep what's earning its place (content, SEO equity, brand recognition), and rebuild the rest with modern design and real performance behind it.

How do revisions work? +

Every project includes structured rounds of feedback at each stage — design, build, and pre-launch review — so nothing moves forward without your sign-off. Revision scope is agreed on up front in the quote, and we're always happy to talk through additional rounds if your needs shift mid-project.

What do you need from me to get started? +

A clear sense of your goals, any existing brand assets (logo, colors, content), and access to your current site or hosting if we're rebuilding. If you don't have content or photography ready yet, that's fine — we can help you get there as part of the process.

Do you only work with local Florida businesses? +

We're based in Florida, but we work with clients nationwide. Everything is handled remotely with clear communication throughout — calls, shared documents, and regular check-ins — so distance has never been a problem.

How do I get in touch? +

Head to the contact page and tell us a bit about what you're working on. We read every message ourselves and typically reply within a business day or two with next steps and, if it's a fit, a custom quote.
